description=[h3]Virtual Stacks[/h3]
description=Project Zomboid hard-caps item stacks at the engine level (e.g. 20 Nails, 10 rounds of ammunition) and never merges two full piles. GridInventory's virtual stacks group compatible adjacent piles into a single cell, merging up to the real engine limit (100 Nails, 50 rounds) and showing the combined unit count right in the stack badge. This is not a stacking hack — the underlying engine piles stay completely vanilla, so everything works with any other mod, and the merged state is fully synced in multiplayer.
